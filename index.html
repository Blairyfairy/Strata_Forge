01 / MARKET LANDSCAPE

Competitive intelligence

Scenario: a small commercial building operator wants to reduce avoidable electricity use. Compare alternative solution archetypes, not named vendor claims.

Utility portal

Historical consumption and billing visibility. Investigate interval data availability and export access.

Building automation

Equipment monitoring and control. Investigate integration costs and operational permissions.

Energy advisory

Expert interpretation and improvement planning. Investigate turnaround and recurring service costs.

Research starting points: ENERGY STAR Buildings · DOE Building Technologies Office. These are background resources, not validation of the scenario.

04 / DELIVERY & GOVERNANCE

From hypothesis to learning

Discover

Observe facilities staff reviewing utility bills. Test access to interval data.

Prototype

Replay historical data and compare alerts with a simple baseline.

Evaluate

Measure false alerts, investigation time and operator comprehension before expansion.

Proposed safeguards: read-only access, role-based permissions, traceable recommendations and no autonomous equipment control. Actual integration and savings require testing.
